## Overview

Humanic helps you build authentic podcasts by grounding every episode in real conversations from verified experts. The platform combines AI assistance with human oversight so you maintain full creative control while accelerating production.

<Callout kind="info">
  All content in Humanic originates from recorded conversations with founders, investors, and operators. The AI never generates synthetic answers from generic training data.
</Callout>

## How Content Gets Sourced

Humanic sources answers directly from recorded interviews rather than large language models alone. When you ask a question through Ask Humanic, the system retrieves relevant moments from verified conversations and surfaces the original speaker context.

This approach delivers three advantages:

- Answers stay grounded in authentic dialogue
- You can always trace every insight back to its source
- Experts retain ownership of their spoken contributions

## The AI Co-Creation Workflow

You stay in control at every stage while the AI handles repetitive tasks.

<Steps>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Draft" icon="sparkles">

## Human-in-the-Loop Controls

You can pause the AI at any point and take direct control. Every suggestion includes a "Show source" link so you can listen to the original clip before approving it.

## Data Privacy & Ownership

<Expandable title="Content ownership" default-open="true">
  You own every episode you create. Humanic never claims rights to your final shows or the underlying segments you select.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Expert consent">
  Voices must explicitly opt in before their conversations appear in search results. You can filter results to only include experts who have granted commercial use rights.
</Expandable>

Humanic stores all chat history and draft episodes under your account. You can delete any conversation or project at any time.
