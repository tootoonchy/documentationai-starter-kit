## Latest Releases

<Update label="2024-11-05" description="v2.1.0" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## New Features

- Ask Humanic now processes complex founder and investor queries with deeper context retention across multi-turn conversations.
- Humanic Studio introduces custom segment templates that let you build episode structures in seconds.

## Improvements

- Voice generation speed increased by 40 percent for all premium voices.
- Episode export options expanded to include direct integration with major podcast hosting platforms.

</Update>

<Update label="2024-10-22" description="v2.0.5" tags={["improvement", "bugfix"]}>

- Fixed performance lag when loading large episode projects with multiple segments.

</Update>
