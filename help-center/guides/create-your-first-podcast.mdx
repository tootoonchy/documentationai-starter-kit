---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new episode">
    Click "New Episode" on your Humanic dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record or upload audio">
    Use our built-in recorder, or upload your audio file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collaborate with AI co-host">
    Add your talking points and let Humanic suggest edits or questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Review, then click "Publish". Humanic distributes to podcast platforms.
  </Step>
</Steps>
