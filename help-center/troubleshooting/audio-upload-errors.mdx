---
title: Audio File Won't Upload
description: Fix upload issues in Humanic.
---

## Symptoms

- Audio stalls during upload
- Error: "File type unsupported" or "Upload failed"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check supported formats">
    MP3, WAV, and M4A only. Convert your file if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File size limits">
    Max 200MB per file. Compress large audio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry using a different browser">
    Chrome works best with Humanic uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>
