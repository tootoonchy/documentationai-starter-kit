## Overview

Sign up, pick a podcast concept, and let Humanic guide episode creation.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://humanic.epiclabs.com` and click "Sign Up"
2. Fill in your podcast details
3. Select AI co-creation partners (optional)
4. Follow the onboarding prompts

## Need help?

Email `support@epiclabs.com` or visit our community for tips.
